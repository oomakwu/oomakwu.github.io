One path, six stages

  1. 01Identify and define valueProblem, beneficiary, baseline method
  2. 02Assess and decideScore, tier the risk, record the decision
  3. 03PrepareControls, change plan, baseline taken
  4. 04Implement and adoptEvaluation gate, then a bounded pilot
  5. 05Monitor and governRe-test on every material change
  6. 06Realize valueMeasure against the stage-1 baseline

Every piece of work hangs off this path. Each stage ends in a decision someone owns, and a use case that isn't delivering gets stopped on purpose.

Selected work

Practical AI Governance for Regulated Infrastructure

A proportionate, evidence-based governance model for organizations where a wrong answer can reach work on energized equipment. Three-factor risk tiers, evaluation results that end in a GO / HOLD decision rather than a score, and named decision rights.

Shows: responsible AI and risk assessment, governance operating model, judgement on external frameworks (NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, Treasury Board ADM Directive) used as lenses, not compliance claims.
